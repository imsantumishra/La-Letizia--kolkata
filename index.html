Our Story

A Taste of Italy,
Close to Home

At La Letizia, every dish tells a story — of sun-drenched Tuscan hills, of recipes passed down through generations, and of the simple joy of gathering around a table with those you love.

Our chefs honor the traditions of regional Italian cooking, sourcing the finest ingredients to craft pasta, sauces, and entrées that capture the soul of Italy. From our wood-fired oven to your glass of Chianti, every detail is considered.

— Benvenuti a La Letizia
